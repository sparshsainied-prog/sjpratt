The 716, book one of the Meliora Chronicles

In Meliora, your light decides your life

In a futuristic society where men are second-class citizens and only binary gender norms are acceptable, two people have to decide what kind of world they want to live in.

Olivia

Destined to be the future leader of Meliora. She’s smart, rich, and innovative, and she has a pink Identifeye light.

Andy

Destined to be a waiter and househusband. His blue Identifeye light prevents him from pursuing his dream of becoming an engineer. After all, who ever heard of a male engineer?

But when Andy’s life becomes entangled with Olivia’s and he gets the chance to prove himself on the female stage, everything starts to change.

Will they do what is expected or what is right? And will the wrong choice spell disaster?
